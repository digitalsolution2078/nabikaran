# Nabikaran — pre-launch audit (21 Oct 2026)

Scope: full review of the live codebase (main `455375c`, 28,300 lines, 363 tests green, build green) as DevOps + product + full-stack: security, reliability, UX on desktop and mobile (EN + NE), SEO/conversion, product gaps for the Nepal market. Evidence is in the repo paths cited; screenshots were taken from a local production build.

Legend: 🔴 fix before launch · 🟠 launch week · 🟡 first month · ✅ already good

## 1. Executive summary

The platform is technically solid: every admin route is permission-checked, cookies are HttpOnly/Secure/SameSite, auth endpoints are rate-limited, security headers are set, the message pipeline is idempotent, hot tables are indexed, and the deploy backs up the database first. What is weak is the **first five minutes for a new customer**, the **year-2 loop** (what happens on and after renewal day), **measurement** (no analytics at all), **SEO sharing** (no social image, no structured data), and a **backup gap** (backups only happen on deploy).

Top 10 by impact:

1. 🔴 New customers start at **−1 credits** with a "Negative balance" warning on their very first screen (sign-in fee charged on first OTP; `src/lib/auth/otp.ts:141`, default `signin.fee_credits = 1`).
2. 🔴 **"Mark as renewed" is Pro-only** (`src/lib/core/reminders.ts:710`). The one action every customer must take once a year is paywalled; free non-repeating records just die as "Expired".
3. 🔴 **No follow-up after expiry.** Day-0 SMS says "renew on time" and then nothing. No "Did you renew?" prompt, no late-fine ladder. Vehicle-tax template has **no day-0 reminder** at all (`default_offsets {30d,7d,1d}`).
4. 🔴 **Database backups only run at deploy time** (`deploy/deploy.sh:60`). A month without deploys = a month without backups. No restore drill documented as done.
5. 🔴 **Zero measurement**: no analytics, no funnel events (sign-up → first record → first top-up), no UTM capture. We will be launching blind.
6. 🟠 **Sharing/SEO**: no OpenGraph image, no JSON-LD, no canonical URL on the landing page. Shared links on Facebook/Viber/WhatsApp show no preview.
7. 🟠 **Dashboard empty state** is six "0" cards; the add-record form on mobile shows a blank custom form first with templates below the fold.
8. 🟠 **Customer-facing English leaks** in Nepali mode: SEO landing pages (`/renewal-reminder/*`), Terms, receipts (~68 hardcoded strings). Unbranded default 404 page.
9. 🟠 **Trust signals missing** on landing/footer: company name & PAN, payment logos, contact, testimonials.
10. 🟡 **Nepal template coverage**: ward business registration, trade licence, pollution/green sticker, NEA/water bill, SIM validity, bank KYC, SSF are missing; "repeat yearly" is off by default for vehicle/licence/insurance.

## 7. Product gaps (Nepal market)

- 🔴 Year-2 loop: free "Mark as renewed" (keeps the record alive, rolls the date), post-expiry ladder (push +3 d, SMS +7 d, push +30 d; vehicle tax +80 d "fine rises soon").
- 🔴 Offsets: vehicle-tax add day-0; insurance add 3 d and a +1 d "policy lapsed" warning.
- 🟠 "Repeat yearly" default ON for bluebook, tax, licence, insurance templates.
- 🟠 8 missing templates: ward business registration, trade/firm licence, pollution check, NEA electricity, KUKL water, SIM validity, bank KYC, SSF contribution (+ SEO pages).
- 🟡 Office/fee info card per template (where to go, documents, approx fee, late-fine rule).
- 🟡 "Remind my father/mother" — second verified number on a personal account (OTP consent), without needing Business approval. Unlocks diaspora.
- 🟡 Free monthly digest push/email ("3 records expire this month") for retention between renewals.
- 🟡 Business Pro (lapsed-client report, bulk renewed, VAT invoice, bulk SMS packs) — 90-day item.

## 8. Work plan

Decided by the owner (21 Oct): the sign-in fee stays as designed (it stops OTP abuse that costs real SMS credits); everything else proceeds one batch at a time. Done so far: Batch A (except the fee, by design) and Batch B.

**Batch A — Launch blockers (1–2 days)**
1. First sign-in free; sign-in fee from the second sign-in only.
2. "Mark as renewed" free for everyone (cost/history detail stays Pro); roll the date for yearly records.
3. Post-expiry follow-up ladder + vehicle-tax day-0 + insurance 3 d/+1 d; migration updating `document_templates.default_offsets`.
4. Nightly backup cron + off-site copy + restore drill doc.
5. First-party funnel events + admin "Funnel" card (sign-ups → first record → first top-up → Pro).

**Batch B — First impression & sharing (1–2 days)**
6. Dashboard empty-state onboarding; mobile add-record shows templates first.
7. OpenGraph image, JSON-LD, canonical, `lang` by preference; branded 404.
8. Translate the 68 hardcoded strings; footer trust block (company, PAN, contact, payment logos).
9. Touch-target and `btn-sm` sizing on mobile.

**Batch C — Nepal coverage (1 day)**
10. 8 new templates + SEO pages; "repeat yearly" default ON for vehicle/licence/insurance.
11. Office/fee info card per template (EN/NE copy).

**Batch D — Month 1**
12. "Remind my father/mother" second number; free monthly digest; settings regrouping; PWA install button; CSP tightening; Sentry.

Not touched without explicit approval: pricing values, Pro price, sign-in fee amount beyond "first sign-in free", production `.env`, other Docker apps on the VPS.
